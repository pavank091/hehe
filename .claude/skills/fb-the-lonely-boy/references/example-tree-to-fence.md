# Worked example (approved by the user): tree reference → fence-post variation

## Reference breakdown (15 s, 720×1170, 24 fps)

| Time | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | Tight close-up. He covers ~70% of the frame, shoulders cut off | Grey stick figure at night, his left hand pressed on a big tree trunk. Head bowed, heavy-lidded eyes, tears on his lower lids |
| 2–3.5 s | Same, no camera move | He slowly turns his head toward the viewer. A tear drops and his right hand starts to rise |
| 3.5–5 s | Same | He brings both hands together in prayer at his chest and his eyes close |
| 5–8 s | Same | A warm glow starts at his hands and turns his whole body cream. The tree bark turns warm too. A small smile appears around 7.5 s |
| 8–9.5 s | Very slight drift back | He holds still in prayer, smiling |
| 9.5–15 s | Fast zoom-out | The camera pulls far back to show him tiny and glowing under a big lone tree on a dark hill, with a starry sky and a pink horizon |

Overlays: caption text across the top 25% of every frame, plus a "Dola AI" watermark. For most of the video the character covers the screen, and the only camera move is the zoom-out at the end.

## How the variation differs
- **The camera goes the opposite way:** it starts wide and pushes in slowly the whole time.
- **He never covers the screen:** at the closest point he's about half the frame width.
- **New setting:** an old wooden fence post at dusk instead of a big tree, and fireflies instead of the plain glow.
- **The top quarter stays empty sky** for the user's own caption text.

## Prompt

Create a 10-second VERTICAL 9:16 video, 24 fps, in the style of a soft 2D hand-drawn cartoon: flat colours, clean even dark outlines, gentle cel shading, and a painted, slightly blurred background. One continuous shot with NO cuts.

CHARACTER (identical in every frame):
* A simple cartoon stick-figure man. Large, perfectly round head, about as wide as his shoulders. Slim, soft, rounded body with a slight hunch. Thin short neck. Long thin arms and legs with smooth joints. Mitten-like hands with a thumb and simple finger lines. Small rounded feet.
* No clothes, no hair, no nose, no ears.
* Skin: flat blue-grey (#888ea3) with a thin pale lavender rim light (#c9cee6) along his LEFT edge and a soft darker blue-grey band (#6d7690) along his RIGHT edge.
* Outline: clean, even, dark slate grey (#363a45), the same thickness everywhere.
* Face placed LOW on the head: two white almond eyes with heavy, flat, drooping upper lids covering the top half, tiny black pupils just under the lids, soft grey bags under the eyes, thin worried eyebrows, and a tiny short mouth line turned slightly down.
* Personality: tired, quiet, gentle, holding in his sadness. Small, slow, subtle movements only. Never bouncy or exaggerated.

SETTING:
* Late dusk on a lonely grassy hilltop. He stands beside an old, weathered wooden fence: one thick fence post with two loose rails running off to the right.
* Sky: deep navy-blue at the top fading to lavender and a soft pink glow at the horizon, with the first few faint stars. Long thin dark clouds drift slowly from left to right.
* Far behind him, soft blurred rolling hills and one tiny distant tree on the horizon.
* Tall dark grass and a few wild grass stalks around his feet sway gently in the wind.
* No other people, no animals, no buildings.

TIMELINE:
* 0–2.5 s: WIDE SHOT. He stands beside the fence post in a 3/4 view facing slightly left, with his left hand resting on top of the post. His head is bowed, his shoulders are slumped, and pale blue tears (#a7cef3) shine on his lower eyelids. He breathes slowly, with a slight rise and fall of his shoulders.
* 2.5–4.5 s: He blinks slowly and heavily. One tear slides down his cheek, leaving a thin wet trail. He slowly lifts his eyes and then his head toward the sky. His eyebrows tilt up in the middle, worried and hopeful.
* 4.5–6.5 s: He slowly takes his hand off the post and brings both hands together in prayer in front of his chest, palms pressed flat and fingers pointing up. His eyes gently close into soft downward curves, and thin tear trails stay on both cheeks.
* 6.5–8.5 s: A small, soft, warm golden glow appears between his praying palms and slowly spreads over his chest, arms and face. His skin gently warms to a pale cream tone and his left rim light turns warm cream. A few tiny glowing fireflies rise slowly around him. The warm light softly touches the edge of the fence post. A faint warm blush appears under his eyes.
* 8.5–10 s: A small, peaceful, closed-mouth smile appears. The tears stay on his cheeks. He holds still in prayer, calm and at peace, while the grass sways and the fireflies drift.

CAMERA:
* One slow, smooth, continuous PUSH-IN (dolly forward) for the full 10 seconds. The move toward him should be clearly noticeable, and it eases out gently at the end.
* START: a wide shot. His whole body, head to feet, plus the fence post and grass, sits in the lower half of the frame, and he takes up about one-third of the frame height. The upper half of the frame is open dusk sky.
* END: a medium shot from his waist up, showing his whole round head, his praying hands and the top of the fence post. He is centred in the frame.
* Even at the closest point he takes up only about HALF of the frame width. Keep visible sky and blurred hills on both his left and right sides (at least 20% of the frame width each).
* Keep the TOP 25% of the frame as open, empty sky for the WHOLE video. His head must always stay below it and must never touch or be cut off by any frame edge.
* Never get closer than waist-up: no close-ups, no face-filling shots. No shake, no rotation, no zoom jumps, no cuts.

LIGHTING AND COLOUR:
* 0–6.5 s: cool, dim, melancholic dusk in blues and purples.
* 6.5–10 s: warm golden light from his hands gradually becomes the main light, creating a hopeful, peaceful mood. Change slowly, never suddenly.

MOTION QUALITY:
* Smooth, slow, gentle animation, like a calm emotional animated short. No jitter, no morphing, no flicker. His proportions and face design never change.

AUDIO:
* A soft, slow, muffled sad piano melody in a minor key with gentle reverb, plus a faint evening wind and quiet crickets.
* The music gently warms, with a soft major chord, when the golden glow appears.
* NO voice, dialogue, singing or narration.

DO NOT INCLUDE:
* any text, captions, subtitles, letters, numbers, watermarks or logos;
* extra characters, realistic humans, 3D rendering or photorealism;
* clothes, hair or a nose on the character;
* a large tree beside the character;
* close-ups, the character filling the screen, or his head or hands cut off by the frame;
* fast motion, cuts or scene changes.
